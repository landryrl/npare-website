About Us

North Pinellas Advocates for Racial Equity

NPARE is a community organization serving North Pinellas County, Florida, focused on advocacy and practical support for the community's needs.

Our Focus

Advocating for our community's needs

NPARE works with residents, faith communities, and local professionals across North Pinellas County to advance racial equity and support the community — including small business owners who are building and growing local businesses.

1

Advocacy

We advocate for fairness and opportunity for residents throughout North Pinellas County.

2

Small Business Support

Through our free Minority Business Accelerator (MBA) info sessions, we connect small business owners with resources to grow, scale, and sustain their businesses. See the MBA Program page for details.

3

Community Partnership

We partner with local organizations and venues — including community institutions like the Pinellas County African American Museum — to bring programming directly to the community.

NPARE Program

Minority Business Accelerator (MBA)

Free informational sessions for small business owners in the North Pinellas County area on NPARE's Minority Business Accelerator — a program designed to help you grow, scale, and sustain your business. (This MBA is NPARE's Minority Business Accelerator, not a Master of Business Administration degree.)

About the Minority Business Accelerator

Why attend an MBA session?

For small business owners · North Pinellas County area
  • Access real growth tools — learn how the Minority Business Accelerator can sharpen your business, financial, and leadership skills.
  • Grow your network — connect with fellow entrepreneurs and community leaders across North Pinellas County.
  • Get your questions answered — speak directly with program representatives about enrollment and requirements.
  • It's free — light refreshments provided, no cost to attend.

About the Program

What to expect

These free sessions are designed for small business owners across North Pinellas County who want to strengthen how they run and grow their business. NPARE hosts them in partnership with local venues and community leaders, with program representatives on hand to answer questions about enrollment and requirements.
